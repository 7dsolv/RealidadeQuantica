The four .f32 files contain the original, unchanged tensor bytes extracted
from anomaly_net.pt. Serialization was changed to raw little-endian float32
so MATLAB and Fortran can read the data without executing Python or pickle.
reference.json transcribes the published numeric test vector.

The KONAET adapters, quantization, local evaluation and circuit prototype
are separate project contributions. AnomalyNet was not trained by KONAET.
The author's reported accuracy and MCU timings are not KONAET measurements.
